Book a call

Engagements typically start with a fixed-scope audit of your agent architecture: one to two weeks, ending in a written assessment and a roadmap. Larger implementation work is scoped from there. This call is where we figure out fit.

On the call

Thirty minutes. You bring the problem: what you're building, where agents fit, what's not working. I'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit for how I work, and either way you'll leave with my read on your architecture and a suggested next step.

This call is for you if
